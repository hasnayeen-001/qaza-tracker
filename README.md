🕋 Automated Qaza Prayer Tracker & Printable Generator

A clean, privacy-focused Streamlit web application designed for non-coders and individuals looking to calculate, adjust, and track their missed (*Qaza*) obligatory prayers over a lifetime or customized multi-year range.

The system calculates missed prayers, applies a customizable **Safety Margin**, divides obligations across the 5 daily Islamic prayers (**Fajr, Zuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha**), and generates a print-ready, high-quality **ReportLab PDF** equipped with custom rounded-corner checkbox grids.

**Now available on 3 platforms! Choose how you want to use it:**
- ☁️ **Option 1: Live Cloud Version** — Use the hosted Streamlit app online via the link above.
- 🌐 **Option 2: Standalone Browser Version** — Download ([index.html](index.html)) file and double-click it to run it instantly offline in any web browser.
- 🐍 **Option 3: Local Python Server** — Clone the repository and run `streamlit run app.py` on your machine.

## 📖 How to Use

**Step 1: Select Year Range & History**

   * Choose your starting and ending years **(Mandatory)**.

   * Edit the **Adjusted Days** column (default 365) and **Avg Wakt Prayed** column (0.0 to 5.0) for each year **(Optional)**.

**Step 2: Review Summary & Set Safety Margin**

   * Inspect calculated missed prayers.

   * Adjust your **Final Safety Target** to round up your target for peace of mind **(MANDATORY!!!)**.

**Step 3: Allocate Lifetime Target**

   * Distribute your total Safety Target across **Fajr, Zuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha** **(Mandatory)**.

   * Ensure the gap reaches zero so the success banner appears.

**Step 4: Generate Printable PDF**

   * Click **Download Printable PDF Tracker** to download your multi-page PDF logbook ready for printing.

## 🔒 Privacy & Data Policy

This app runs entirely locally or in your private session. No personal prayer data or log histories are stored on external servers or database storage systems.
